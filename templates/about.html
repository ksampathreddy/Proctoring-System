Our Mission

The AI Proctoring System is designed to maintain the integrity of online examinations by leveraging artificial intelligence to monitor test-takers in real-time. Our system ensures that exams are conducted fairly and securely, providing educational institutions with reliable remote assessment tools.

How It Works

Our system uses advanced computer vision and audio analysis to monitor exam sessions. The AI continuously analyzes camera feed for face presence and detects any suspicious audio activity, automatically flagging potential violations to maintain exam integrity.

Privacy & Security

We take privacy seriously. All video and audio data is processed in real-time and is not stored on our servers. The system only analyzes the data to detect violations and does not retain any personal information after the exam session ends.
